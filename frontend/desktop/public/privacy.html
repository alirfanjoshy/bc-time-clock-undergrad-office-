BC TIME CLOCK

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

BC Time Clock helps the Undergraduate Recruitment team record work hours, review attendance, and prepare timesheets. This notice describes information handled by the Time Clock app.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: name, email address, employee identifier, department, account role, account status, and a hashed password. If you enable an authenticator, the app also stores information needed to verify its codes.
  • Attendance information: clock-in and clock-out times, work dates, hours worked, notes, timesheets, and records of administrative corrections.
  • Security and activity information: sign-in sessions, account and administrative actions, and, where recorded, IP addresses and browser or device information.
  • Browser storage: a generated device identifier used during clock-in and any profile photo you choose in the web app. The web profile photo is stored in that browser's local storage.

How information is used

The app uses this information to manage accounts, authenticate users, record attendance, calculate work hours, generate reports, support password recovery, and maintain security and an audit history. Authorized office administrators can manage accounts and access attendance information and reports as permitted by their role.

Gmail and password-reset emails

When enabled, the Gmail connection lets the app send password-reset messages from its designated sending account. Only the person managing that account authorizes Google access; employees do not need to connect their own Google accounts.

The connection requests the gmail.send permission to send email. It does not request permission to read the inbox, contacts, or existing messages. Google processes the recipient's email address and message content, including the reset link, to deliver the email. Sent messages may remain in the sending account.

The Gmail connection requires authorization credentials stored on the server, not in employees' browsers. The account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections; doing so stops password-reset delivery through that connection.

For this connection, Google user data will be used only to provide the password-reset email feature, not for advertising, sale, or training AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Service providers and external content

The website runs on Render. The deployment setup uses Neon for account and attendance storage, and Google processes password-reset emails when the Gmail connection is enabled. These providers process information needed to provide their services. The app also displays a Google Maps office map; loading it connects your browser to Google, which can receive connection information such as your IP address. Following an external link is subject to that service's own privacy practices.

Downloaded timesheet reports are also stored wherever the person downloading them saves or shares them.

Storage, security, and retention

The app stores passwords as hashes and uses role-based access controls. Password-reset links expire after 15 minutes and become invalid after a password change. No system can guarantee complete security.

Account and attendance records are kept long term. The current app has no automatic deletion schedule, and disabling an account does not erase its history. Records remain stored unless an administrator arranges their removal or the underlying storage is deleted. This is not a guarantee of permanent storage. Contact the Time Clock administrator to ask about retention or request deletion; the administrator must consider any applicable office recordkeeping requirements. Provider backups, server logs, downloaded reports, and sent emails may have separate retention periods.

Browser-stored information remains until it is replaced or the browser's site data is cleared. Clearing site data can affect device registration.

Questions and requests

To ask about your information, request a correction, or discuss account closure or deletion, contact the Time Clock administrator at undergradbctimeclock@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or reset links in your message. The administrator should explain any records that must be retained.

Changes to this notice

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Changes to the Gmail connection's data use will be disclosed before requesting any additional Google permissions.
